Are all your plots DTCP or RERA approved?

Yes. Every layout we list carries DTCP approval, and projects above the RERA threshold are RERA registered. We share approval numbers before your site visit.

Do you charge for site visits?

No. Site visits are free, including pick-up and drop within Vellore and Katpadi.

Can you help me get a home loan?

Yes. We work with leading banks and housing finance companies and help you with eligibility, documents and approval.

Which areas in Vellore do you cover?

Katpadi, Gandhi Nagar, Sathuvachari, Bagayam, Brammapuram, Thorapadi, Kangeyanallur and surrounding areas near VIT and CMC.

I want to sell my property. Can you list it?

Yes. Share your property details through the enquiry form — our team will visit, verify documents and list it for qualified buyers.
